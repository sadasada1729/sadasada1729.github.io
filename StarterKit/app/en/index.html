CORE FEATURES

Choose the sound and timing for your practice

Select your sound and start method. Use Starter Kit for individual repetition, team sessions, or starts directed by a coach.

Choose voice cues or start sounds

Select human voice cues or start sounds for your session. Cues such as “On Your Marks,” “Set,” and a pistol sound help you practice the start sequence.

Start at set intervals

Set the time before a cue and the intervals between cues. Give yourself time to get ready and repeat the same sequence at a timing you choose.

Start at random intervals

Randomize the intervals between cues to make the start harder to anticipate. Practice listening for the sound and reacting to it.

Start manually

Trigger the start manually at a time you choose. Coaches can check that athletes are ready before giving the cue during individual or team practice.

Reaction-time measurement

Measure your reaction at the start and use the result to review your training.

Video review

Record the start movement, then review your body position, form, and response to the sound.

QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

What is Starter Kit?

Starter Kit is an iPhone app for track sprint starts and swimming start practice. It plays human voice cues and start sounds, with set intervals, random timing, or manual starts. Basic features are free to use.

Can I use it for swimming as well as track?

Yes. Starter Kit supports swimming start practice as well as track sprint starts. Swimming start sounds are also available, so you can practice reacting to a swimming start cue. Choose set intervals, random timing, or manual starts to suit your session.

Can I choose human voice cues or start sounds?

Yes. You can choose human voice cues or start sounds. Cues such as “On Your Marks,” “Set,” and a pistol sound let you practice the start sequence.

What start methods are available?

There are three methods: set the timing, use random intervals, or trigger the start manually. Use them for repeating a sequence, reacting to unpredictable cues, or receiving a cue from a coach.

Can I set the time before the start?

Yes. Set the time before a cue and the intervals between “On Your Marks,” “Set,” and the pistol sound. This gives you time to get ready or repeat practice at a consistent timing.

What can I practice with random starts?

Random intervals make the start timing harder to predict. Use them to practice listening and reacting to the sound instead of moving to a fixed rhythm.

Can a coach trigger the start manually?

Yes. A coach can check that athletes are ready, then trigger the start manually at a chosen moment. This works for team practice and individual coaching.

Is Starter Kit free to use?

Yes. Starter Kit’s basic features are free to use. Check the App Store and the information in the app for in-app purchases and the availability of individual features.

Can I practice starts on my own?

Yes. Set intervals or random starts let you practice without another person giving the cue. Manual starts are also available for sessions with a coach or team.

Can it measure reaction time?

Yes. The app can measure your reaction at the start so you can use the result when reviewing a training session.

Can I review my start on video?

Yes. Record the start movement and review your form and response to the sound afterward.
