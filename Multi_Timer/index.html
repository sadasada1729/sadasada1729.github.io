Who it’s for

Whose time do you need to track?

Built especially for coaches and team managers timing several athletes on their own. Choose a mode for team practice, work and rest intervals, or several everyday tasks.

Coaches and team managers

Time several athletes in track or swimming. Identify each stopwatch by name and color, and group them to start or record laps together.

PE teachers and fitness trainers

Students and cooks

Stopwatch features

Manage the timing and the results

Individual and group controls

Use START, STOP, LAP, and RESET for each stopwatch. Group stopwatches to start, stop, or record laps together.

Lap and split times

A lap measures one segment; a split measures the total time since the start. Record both to review each athlete’s pace and passing times.

Names, colors, and order

Change each stopwatch’s name and color, and choose how many appear on screen. Rearrange them manually or sort automatically by rank.

Saved results in a calendar

Open DATA and save with a title, then revisit past records in the calendar. You can also edit recorded times afterward.

CSV and image sharing

Share results as CSV files or images, or copy them for organization and analysis in Excel or Google Sheets.

Volume button controls

Enable volume button controls in settings to start or stop stopwatches in sequence with the volume down button.

Choose a timing mode

Match the mode to the task

How to use Multi-Timer

Measure, save, and review

  1. Choose a mode and prepare

    Swipe horizontally to choose a mode. Name and color your stopwatches, and group them when needed.

  2. Start, record laps, and stop

    Tap START to begin, LAP to record passing times, and STOP to finish. Use RESET when preparing a new measurement.

  3. Save and share from DATA

    Open DATA, choose the save action, and enter a title. Revisit records in the calendar, share a CSV file or image, or copy the results.

Multi-Timer frequently asked questions

Who is Multi-Timer for?

Multi-Timer is an iPhone and iPad app for operating several stopwatches or timers on one device. It is especially useful for coaches and team managers timing multiple athletes. Runners, swimmers, cyclists, PE teachers, fitness trainers, students, and cooks can also use its timing modes.

How many stopwatches can I run at once?

You can run 20+ stopwatches at once, depending on your device, and adjust how many appear on screen. The same number is not guaranteed on every device.

What is the difference between a lap and a split?

A lap is the time for one segment. A split is the total elapsed time from the start. Multi-Timer can record both.

Can I start athletes together and stop them individually?

Yes. Each stopwatch has individual start, stop, and reset controls. You can also group stopwatches to start, stop, or record laps together.

Can I save results and use them in Excel or Google Sheets?

Yes. Open DATA, choose the save action, and enter a title to revisit results in the calendar. You can edit times, share CSV files or images, and copy results for organization and analysis in Excel or Google Sheets.

Can I time a start and finish in different locations?

Online Timer links stopwatches on different devices through a shared room ID. It is useful when the start and finish are far apart or several people help with timing. A stable internet connection is required. Online Timer is available as a separate one-time in-app purchase for continued use, or temporarily by watching an ad.

Can I record laps without watching the screen?

NoLook Timer lets you tap as athletes pass and assign marks to athletes afterward. You can adjust times, and laps are calculated automatically. The volume down button can also start timing or record a lap. NoLook Timer is available as a separate one-time in-app purchase for continued use, or temporarily by watching an ad.

How does photo finish timing work?

Photo finish links the stopwatch to video playback. Use slow motion, fast-forward, and pause to inspect finishes or swimming touches, then manually record times for each athlete. Photo finish is available as a separate one-time in-app purchase for continued use, or temporarily by watching an ad.

Will cooking or study timers sound while the screen is locked?

On iOS 26 or later, Timer For Daily Use can sound while another app is open or the screen is locked when you allow alarms, including in Silent mode or Focus. When regular notifications are used, sound behavior depends on notification settings.

Does the HIIT or Tabata timer send background alerts?

Timer For Training does not support background sounds or notifications. Keep the app open to follow work and rest changes and hear alerts. You can repeat a sequence up to 9,999 times and save it for reuse. Toggle audio with the sound button and change vibration in Settings.

Is Multi-Timer free, and are the extras subscriptions?

All basic features are free. Photo finish, Online Timer, and NoLook Timer are each one-time in-app purchases for continued use. You can also use these features temporarily by watching an ad without purchasing them. Ad removal is a separate one-time purchase. Check the App Store or the in-app purchase screen for current prices.

Does Multi-Timer work on iPhone and iPad?

Yes. Multi-Timer is for iPhone and iPad and supports iPad multitasking. Check the App Store for current operating system and device requirements.

Can I use Multi-Timer outside sports?

Yes. In addition to cooking and study countdowns, reported uses include game time allowances and multiple workplace tasks. It is also used in keirin, car and motorboat races, and is widely deployed on organization-managed devices using MDM.
